# SwipeLog Product Roadmap and Monetization Plan

## Positioning

SwipeLog should launch as a free, mobile-first movie journal that helps people
understand and express their movie taste. The first release should keep the
current feature set free: search, logging, ratings, watchlist, library, diary,
favorites, basic discovery, tier lists, account sync, and cloud backup.

The long-term premium model should not take existing free value away. Premium
should unlock deeper, more powerful versions of the same product promise:
better filtering, richer taste analysis, stronger personalization, more useful
widgets, and more polished sharing.

Primary positioning:

> Discover your movie taste.

Supporting messages:

- Track what you watch. Remember what you love.
- Your film history, beautifully organized.
- Turn your movie year into shareable stories.
- Build your watchlist, rate your favorites, and see your taste evolve.

## Free Core

The free product should remain strong enough to grow the audience and build a
habit before monetization becomes prominent.

Free features should include:

- Browse and search movies.
- Log watched movies with ratings and notes.
- Save movies to watchlist.
- Keep diary, favorites, custom lists, and tier lists.
- Use basic discovery and recommendation flows.
- Sync account, profile, movie data, and tier lists through cloud state.
- Use the basic monthly or yearly Wrapped experience when it ships.
- Share basic Wrapped cards and basic tier-list images.

The free tier's job is growth, retention, and organic sharing. It should feel
complete, not like a trial.

## Premium Strategy

Premium should launch only after the product has enough audience and retention
signals. The first premium identity should be a power-user plan.

Recommended premium pillars:

1. Advanced filtering
   - Filter search, watchlist, Browse, and Discover by streaming platform,
     runtime, genre, release year, rating, provider availability, watchlist
     status, watched status, and favorites.
   - Let users prioritize recommendations by the platforms they actually use.

2. Streaming-aware discovery
   - Use watch provider data to rank Browse and Discover rows.
   - Add rows such as "Available on your services", "Tonight on your platforms",
     and "High-rated picks you can stream now".

3. Advanced statistics
   - Expand all-time statistics into monthly and yearly views.
   - Add deeper taste analysis: genre trends, rating behavior, rewatch patterns,
     favorite release decades, runtime habits, and day/month activity.
   - Make premium statistics visually richer, easier to share, and more fun to
     revisit.

4. Widgets
   - Start with basic widgets when widget support is introduced.
   - Reserve advanced widgets for premium: Tonight's Pick, watchlist queue,
     yearly stats, quick log, favorite films, and mood-based suggestions.

5. Alternative posters
   - Let premium users choose from alternate poster variants.
   - Prefer official or provider-backed poster alternatives first.
   - Avoid custom uploads and AI poster generation in v1 because they add
     moderation, storage, copyright, and cost risk.

6. Enhanced sharing
   - Keep basic share cards free.
   - Add premium templates, themes, high-quality exports, and richer tier-list
     layouts.

## Wrapped Strategy

Wrapped should be a growth engine first and a premium upsell second.

Free Wrapped:

- Monthly and yearly recap.
- Core cards for watched count, top genres, top-rated films, watch time,
  favorite month, rating distribution, and standout films.
- Story-friendly export formats for Instagram and TikTok.

Premium Wrapped:

- More cards and deeper analysis.
- Custom visual themes.
- High-resolution exports.
- More detailed taste profile and recommendation takeaways.
- Optional archive of prior Wrapped reports.

The free version should be intentionally shareable because it can become
SwipeLog's best organic advertising channel.

## Retention Roadmap

These features should give users regular reasons to return without weakening
the free product. Most of them should start as free engagement loops, with only
advanced controls or premium presentation layers reserved for paid plans later.

Recommended priority:

1. Upcoming Releases from Watchlist
   - Track release dates for movies saved to watchlist.
   - Notify users when a watchlisted movie is coming soon, released, or newly
     relevant.
   - This should build on the existing watchlist and smart notification
     foundation.

2. Continue Your Library
   - Surface incomplete actions in the app.
   - Examples: watched films without ratings, diary entries without notes,
     empty custom lists, imported films that need cleanup, and watchlist items
     missing metadata.

3. Weekly and Monthly Recap
   - Summarize recent activity every week and month.
   - Include watched count, top-rated film, total watch time, favorite genre,
     rating pattern, and notable diary activity.
   - Monthly recap can later feed into the free Wrapped system.

4. Tonight's Pick
   - Recommend one film to watch tonight.
   - Start with watchlist-based picks using rating, runtime, metadata quality,
     and recency.
   - Premium can later add streaming provider, mood, genre, and runtime
     constraints.

5. Taste Profile Update
   - Show small insights when the user's taste shifts.
   - Examples: "You are watching more thrillers lately" or "Your top genre this
     month is drama."
   - These insights should connect Statistics, Discovery, and Wrapped.

6. Smart Lists
   - Generate automatic lists that update as the user's library changes.
   - Examples: Short Watchlist, Highest Rated, Rewatched, Unwatched Classics,
     Weekend Picks, Recent Favorites, and Films With Notes.
   - Custom rule-based smart lists can become a premium feature later.

7. Rewatch Reminder
   - Suggest highly rated older films for rewatch.
   - Prioritize films with strong ratings, favorites, old watch dates, or repeat
     watch history.

8. Seasonal Challenges
   - Add time-limited prompts around cultural moments and seasons.
   - Examples: October Horror Month, Oscar Season, Summer Watchlist, One Movie
     From Each Decade, and 30 Days of Cinema.

9. Friend Recommendation Inbox
   - Create a dedicated place for movies recommended by friends.
   - Recommendations should show who sent them and whether the user watched,
     saved, skipped, or rated the film.

10. Friend Activity
    - Show lightweight updates from friends.
    - Examples: watched films, favorites, shared tier lists, completed
      challenges, and new public lists.
    - This should come after profiles and friend connections are stable.

11. Group Watchlist Updates
    - Notify users when a shared watchlist changes.
    - Examples: new film added, votes changed, a group pick is selected, or a
      movie is marked watched together.
    - This depends on group watchlist and room features.

Retention feature principles:

- Prefer meaningful prompts over generic push notifications.
- Keep basic retention loops free because they build habit.
- Use premium only for advanced filters, deeper controls, richer presentation,
  or larger group limits.
- Make every notification actionable: open a film, add a rating, choose a pick,
  view a recap, or update a list.

## Future Feature Backlog

### Tonight's Pick

Recommend one film from the user's watchlist for tonight.

Free version:

- Simple pick based on watchlist, rating, and availability of metadata.

Premium version:

- Add runtime, mood, streaming provider, genre, and rating constraints.

### Mood-Based Discovery

Add discovery prompts such as:

- Cozy
- Dark thriller
- Short and fun
- Mind-bending
- Emotional
- Critically acclaimed

Premium can combine mood with runtime, platform availability, genre, and user
taste profile.

### Public Profile

Create shareable web profiles that show:

- Top favorites.
- Recent watches.
- Public tier lists.
- Wrapped cards.
- Selected custom lists.

Web should support growth, SEO, and social sharing while the main usage remains
mobile-first.

### Share Cards

Add story-sized cards for:

- Watched film.
- Rating and note.
- Favorite month or year.
- Watchlist milestone.
- Wrapped summary.

Basic templates should be free. Premium should add themes, layouts, and high
quality exports.

### Smart Lists

Automatically generate lists such as:

- Highest rated.
- Rewatched.
- Comfort movies.
- Short watchlist.
- Unwatched classics.
- Recent favorites.
- Films with notes.

Premium can unlock custom rule-based smart lists.

### Friends and Group Picking

Later-stage social features:

- Friend profiles.
- Shared watchlists.
- Taste overlap.
- "What should we watch together?"
- Group voting.

This should come after the personal archive and sharing loops are strong.

## Monetization

Primary model: freemium.

Premium should sell depth, convenience, and expression:

- More powerful discovery.
- Better filters.
- More beautiful statistics.
- Better widgets.
- More polished share outputs.
- More customization.

Secondary model: affiliate revenue.

- Use "Watch now" links when provider data is available.
- Prefer affiliate links over intrusive banner ads.
- Keep the placement useful and transparent.

Advertising model:

- Avoid banner and interstitial ads as the primary business model.
- If ads are introduced, use native editorial placements:
  - Now streaming.
  - Award season picks.
  - Horror week.
  - Sponsored collections.
- Clearly label sponsored content.

## Premium Launch Gates

Premium should not be the first launch focus. Consider launching premium after
several of these signals are healthy:

- Strong weekly active usage.
- Users regularly add watchlist items.
- Users log diary entries after watching.
- Users create or share tier lists.
- Users return to statistics.
- Wrapped cards get shared.
- Cloud sync and account usage are stable.

Until those signals exist, prioritize product quality, onboarding, retention,
and share loops.

## Platform Plan

1. iOS and Android
   - Main product surface.
   - Best for logging, discovery, watchlist, sharing, and notifications.

2. Web
   - Public profiles.
   - Shareable tier-list pages.
   - Wrapped pages.
   - SEO and viral landing pages.

3. Tablet
   - Better tier-list editing.
   - Better statistics exploration.
   - More comfortable library management.

4. Widgets
   - Watchlist.
   - Tonight's Pick.
   - Quick log.
   - Yearly stats.
   - Favorite films.

5. PWA or desktop
   - Import/export.
   - Large list management.
   - Profile editing.

6. TV app
   - Low priority.
   - Potential later use for group browsing and shared watchlist picking.

## Advertising Positioning

Main angle:

> Your movie taste.

Ad concepts:

- A user's watched films transform into a yearly Wrapped report.
- A messy watchlist across chats and notes becomes one clean SwipeLog library.
- Ratings and diary entries turn into a visible taste profile.
- A tier list sparks a friendly argument in a group chat.

Example ad copy:

- Discover your movie taste.
- Track every film you watch.
- Stop losing movie recommendations.
- Your movie year, ready to share.
- Build a watchlist you will actually use.

## Implementation Order

Recommended order after the current app stabilizes:

1. Improve onboarding and first-session activation.
2. Polish Library and Statistics.
3. Build free Wrapped as a viral sharing loop.
4. Add share cards and better tier-list exports.
5. Add platform-aware filtering and provider preferences.
6. Add premium entitlement infrastructure.
7. Launch premium with advanced filters, richer stats, and enhanced sharing.
8. Add widgets and alternative poster selection.
9. Expand to web public profiles and share pages.

## Defaults and Constraints

- Do not remove existing free features when premium launches.
- Keep the first premium plan focused on power users.
- Keep basic Wrapped free.
- Start poster customization with alternate poster selection only.
- Keep mobile as the primary product and web as the sharing/public-profile
  surface.
- Prefer useful affiliate links and native sponsored collections over intrusive
  ads.
