# SwipeLog Google Play listing draft

## App name

SwipeLog

## Short description

Discover films, track what you watch, and share your movie life.

## Full description

SwipeLog is a personal movie discovery and tracking companion built for people who want a faster, more social way to decide what to watch and remember what they have seen.

Discover films with a swipe-first experience shaped by your favorite genres and activity. Search for titles, explore trending and upcoming releases, save films to your watchlist, and open detailed pages with trailers, cast information, recommendations, ratings, and streaming availability.

Keep a complete movie diary with watch dates, ratings, notes, rewatches, favorites, and custom lists. Build tier lists, highlight your favorite four films, and import existing Letterboxd history to get started more quickly.

Connect with friends through public profiles and shared watchlists. Add films together, vote on suggestions, and keep track of what the group has already watched.

Key features:

- Personalized movie discovery
- Search, trending, upcoming, and now-playing films
- Watchlist, diary, ratings, notes, and rewatches
- Custom lists and tier lists
- Letterboxd import
- Public profiles, friends, and shared watchlists
- Optional release reminders
- Account export and deletion controls

Movie metadata and artwork are provided by TMDB. Streaming availability information is powered by JustWatch through TMDB. SwipeLog is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

## App access instructions

SwipeLog requires an email/password account. Reviewers should receive a dedicated verified test account with a populated profile, watch history, watchlist, and one shared watchlist. Do not place production administrator credentials in Play Console.
